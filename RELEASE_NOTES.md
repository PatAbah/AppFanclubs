AppFanclubs 2026.11.13-cat

This calendar release moves AppFanclubs to its dedicated subdomain, restricts club conversations to selected installed-app members, and improves Android scrolling and header spacing.

Download the file for your platform. The clients connect to https://appfanclubs.chillapi.stream/.

Windows: portable EXE. Android: APK. macOS: Apple silicon and Intel DMG/ZIP builds, without Developer ID signing or notarization. iOS Simulator: development archive for a Mac, not an iPhone installer.

Installed-app discovery is available on Windows, Android and macOS. iOS currently supports browsing and account access. Android push delivery is pending configuration.
